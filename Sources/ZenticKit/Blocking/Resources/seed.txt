! Title: Zentic Seed
! Description: First-run protection. Small enough to convert and compile in
!   well under a second, so a cold launch is never blocked waiting for the full
!   EasyList set to download and compile in the background.
! Version: 1
!
! Scope rules for anything added here:
!   - Only endpoints whose sole purpose is advertising, tracking or session
!     replay. This list ships without the community review EasyList gets, so a
!     false positive here is entirely our fault.
!   - No CDN, font, captcha or payment host, ever.
!   - No googletagmanager.com: sites load real functionality through GTM
!     containers, so blocking it breaks pages. EasyPrivacy makes a more careful
!     per-path judgement; wait for it.
!   - No consent-manager scripts. Many sites keep content behind the wall until
!     the CMP answers, so killing the CMP leaves a permanently walled page.
!     Cookie walls are dismissed by autoconsent in the injected bundle instead.
