Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to register a domain?

Registration itself is immediate. The domain is yours as soon as the payment goes through, and in most cases it starts resolving across the internet within an hour, though a small number of endings take up to twenty-four hours to propagate fully to every network.

Can I move a domain I already own?

Yes. You will need the authorisation code from your current registrar and the domain must have been registered for at least sixty days, which is a rule set by the registry rather than by us. Once you start the transfer we will email you at each step, and your website and email keep working throughout — nothing goes dark while the transfer completes.

What happens when my plan renews?

We send a reminder thirty days before any renewal, again at seven days, and once more on the morning of the charge. You can turn automatic renewal off at any point from the account page, and if a renewal catches you by surprise anyway, contact support within thirty days for a full refund.

Do I need technical knowledge to build a site?

No. The great majority of people who build a site with us have never written a line of code and never need to. If you do want to get into the markup there is nothing stopping you, and the export option gives you the whole site as ordinary files you can take elsewhere.

What support is included?

Every plan includes support by phone and chat, staffed around the clock in English, with additional hours in several other languages. There is no separate premium support tier — the person who answers is the same whether you are on the cheapest plan or the most expensive one.
