Shopify is an all-in-one commerce platform that lets you create an online store, accept payments, and sell on social channels and in person—all from one dashboard. Build a professional storefront, manage products and inventory, process orders and shipping, automate marketing and SEO, analyze sales, and run your business from web or mobile—no coding required.
Shopify starts with a free 3-day trial—no credit card required. After your trial, most plans start at just £1 per month for the first 3 months, then switch to standard monthly pricing.

Additionally, you can choose to add POS Pro to your subscription for more advanced point of sale features like staff roles and permissions, exchanges, and flexible customer pickup and delivery options.
Yes. If you ever decide that Shopify isn't the best commerce platform for your business, simply cancel your account.

If you choose a 1- or 3-year Plus subscription, you can cancel when your term commitment expires.
There are no setup fees on any of our plans.

Third-party transaction fees may apply when you use a third-party payment provider (instead of Shopify Payments). The rate depends on your plan.

Shopify charges a small fee to allow you to accept major credit cards, such as Visa and Mastercard, as payment in your store. These fees vary depending on the plan that you choose.
Shopify is available in nearly every country in the world. Some features vary by country—for example, Shopify Payments is available only in certain countries—so pricing and payment options can differ depending on where your business is based.
Yes. Shopify is certified Level 1 PCI DSS compliant, and stores powered by Shopify are PCI compliant by default, which helps keep payment information secure.
Yes. You can use a domain you already own, or buy a new one. Every Shopify store also comes with a free myshopify.com address, and Shopify helps keep your site secure automatically.

If you already have a domain, you can keep it where you bought it and simply connect it to Shopify—or move it to Shopify so everything is managed in one place.
Yes. Shopify includes secure commerce hosting with your Shopify plan, so you don't need to buy separate hosting. Shopify also includes unlimited bandwidth (no bandwidth fees) and provides a free TLS/SSL certificate for domains you add to Shopify.

Shopify is a tech company, not a bank. We partner with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank N.A., Member FDIC.   1. The Balance earnings rate is a reward provided by Shopify and is not interest. The rate is variable, subject to change without notice, and accurate as of August 13, 2026. The reward accrues daily and is paid monthly in the form of a credit to your Balance account. ACH limits may apply. See Reward Terms. Stores on Shopify Plus are rewarded with 3.31%, and all other plans receive 2.29%.

  2. "Cashback" refers to rewards earned as a percentage discount on eligible purchases. Earn 3% cashback as a statement credit on up to US$250,000 of annual eligible purchases in your monthly top spend category—either marketing, fulfillment, or wholesale, and 1% cashback thereafter. Earn 1% cashback on the other two spend categories. Restrictions apply. See Program Terms for details. By default, you are opted in to paying your full statement balance by an automatic debit from your designated bank account within 1 month to avoid fees. You have the option to switch to making daily payments from your sales and take up to 10 months to pay your full statement balance, for a fee. If payment is not received in full within 10 months, a 2% monthly late fee will apply to your past-due balance.

  3. Offers to apply do not guarantee funding. Shopify Capital loans are repaid based on a percentage of daily sales. The maximum repayment term is 18 months, and 2 minimum payments apply. The actual time period within which the loan is repaid may be less than 18 months. Funds through Shopify Capital are added to your account in as little as two business days, if approved.
